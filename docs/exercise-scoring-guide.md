FATIGUE COST;

A blend between 3 different types of fatigue, muscle, nervous system, recovery time.
a rough average of all three to illustrate how "taxing" an exercise may be per set in a regime.


10 = power cleans & snatches, 8-9 = squats/deadlifts, 1-3 = triceps push down.

DIFFICULTY;

Minimum level that a trainer should prescribe an exercise by default. Not if it's possible but the saftey level
a beginner could do it on their own as a main lift in their regime. 

BEGINNER = anyone can do it alone, INTERMEDIATE = a little coaching and experience preferred, ADVANCED = need heavy coaching
and or experience. 

HYPERTROPHY SCORE; 

A scale to classify an exercise on how effective it would be for muscle growth.

10 = dumbbell bench press, 9 = chest fly, 8 = incline dumbbell press, 7 = barbell bench, 1 = snatch

ATHLETIC TRANSFER SCORE;


The scale in which we judge how much and exercise transfers directly into a athletes progression. If the exercise doesn't 
directly lead into the betterment of that athlete it scores low, otherwise it scores high.


10 = cleans/snatches, 9 = sprints/box jumps, 8 = explosive bench/ clap push-ups, 1 = seated wrists curl


STRENGTH SCORE;


Representation of how well an exercise directly improves strength. 


10 = squats/bench, 1 = wrist curl


STABILITY DEMAND;

The stability level required to attempt specific exercises.


3 = pistol squats, 2 = dumbbell bench press, 1 = machine exercises, 0 = seated leg curl
